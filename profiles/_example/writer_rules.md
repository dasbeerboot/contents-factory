# Writer rules

- Open on the consequence, not the setup. First line names a cost, a number or a reversal.
- One claim per post. If a second claim appears, it becomes the next post.
- Every number has a unit and a timeframe.
- Close on something the reader can check tonight. Vary the closing sentence every time.
- Length: aim for the lower half of the platform limit. Cut the last sentence you wrote.
